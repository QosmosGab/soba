Privacy policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

Soba is an after-hours sales desk for produce sellers, provided by Project Peach Inc., doing business as Soba AI ("Soba", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information we collect through the Soba service and the usesoba.com website, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to our customers (the businesses that put Soba on their sales line), to their team members, to the buyers who call or text a customer's line, and to visitors of our website.

1. Information we collect

From customers and their teams

  • Account details: company name, address, billing contact, and the names, phone numbers and email addresses of the people who use Soba.
  • Desk settings: business hours, the phone lines forwarded to Soba, price sheets, volume limits, price rules, and which reps are on call.
  • Communications with us: emails, messages and call notes from onboarding and support.

From buyers who contact a customer's line

  • What the buyer tells us on the call or by text: name, company, callback number, and the details of the order they want to place (product, size, pack, cases, price, pickup or delivery).
  • A written transcript and notes of the conversation. Audio recordings are kept only where the customer has turned recording on and the law allows it; where required, callers are told at the start of the call.

Automatically

  • Call and message metadata: phone numbers, timestamps, duration, delivery status, and the channel used (phone, SMS, WhatsApp, email).
  • Website analytics: pages visited, approximate location, browser and device type. We use this in aggregate to understand how the site is used.

2. How we use information

  • To answer calls and texts, take order requests, read them back, and apply the price rules the customer set.
  • To send callbacks, written confirmations, order cards and morning summaries to buyers and to the customer's team.
  • To route requests that fall outside the rules to the customer's on-call reps and to act on their answer.
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve the service, including reviewing transcripts to correct mistakes.
  • To bill customers and to communicate with them about their account.
  • To comply with law and to respond to lawful requests.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use the content of calls or messages to train models for anyone other than Soba, and we do not use it to market to buyers.

3. Who we share information with

  • The customer. A call or message handled on a customer's line is part of that customer's relationship with the buyer. The customer receives the transcript, the order details and the confirmation.
  • Service providers. Companies that help us run Soba: telephony and messaging carriers, speech recognition and language-model providers, cloud hosting, email delivery, analytics and payment processing. Each is permitted to use the information only to provide its service to us.
  • Legal and safety. Authorities or other parties when required by law, to enforce our terms, or to protect the rights and safety of Soba, our customers or others.
  • Business transfers. A successor in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, under this policy.

4. Messaging consent

Buyers are asked on the call or text whether Soba may call or text them back about their request, and reps opt in on WhatsApp before anything else is sent. Anyone can stop messages by replying STOP. Our SMS and WhatsApp policy describes this in full.

5. Retention

Transcripts, notes and message records are kept for 12 months by default; customers can shorten this in their settings. Audio recordings, where enabled, are kept for 90 days unless the customer sets a shorter period. Account information is kept while the account is active and for up to three years after, for billing and legal purposes. When a customer leaves Soba, their conversation records are returned or deleted on request.

6. Security

Information is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is limited to people and systems that need it to run the service, and access is logged. No service is perfectly secure, and we will notify affected customers of a breach as the law requires.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to a use of it, by writing to hello@usesoba.com. If you are a buyer, we may refer the request to the customer whose line you contacted, because that customer controls the business record.
  • You can opt out of texts and WhatsApp messages by replying STOP.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act or the GDPR, including the right to access, portability and deletion. We honor these requests and will not treat you differently for making one.

8. International transfers

Soba operates from the United States. If you contact a customer's line from outside the United States, your information is processed in the United States under this policy.

9. Children

Soba is a business service and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes

We will post any changes on this page with a new date. If a change is material, we will tell customers by email before it takes effect.
