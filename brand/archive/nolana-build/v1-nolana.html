What Soba Does

Soba takes the order after hours from the first ring to the written confirmation, with your reps in control.

//_01

Answers calls and texts

Picks up your company line after close or after four rings, in English or Spanish. Asks only for what's missing and reads every number back.

//_02

Confirms at your prices

Checks each request against tonight's price sheet and volume limits. Inside the rules, the order is confirmed on the call with a reference number.

//_03

Hands the rest to a rep

Anything outside the rules goes to your on-call reps on WhatsApp: Approve, Counter, Reject or Take over. Soba calls the buyer back with the answer.
