How it works

  1. A buyer calls or texts your company line after hours. The call forwards to Soba.
  2. Soba takes the request: product, size, pack, cases, price, pickup day. It reads every number back and asks permission to call or text back.
  3. If the order meets the prices and volumes you set, it's confirmed on the call with a reference number.
  4. If not, your on-call reps get a WhatsApp card. The first to answer decides.
  5. Soba calls or texts the buyer back with the answer, sends a written confirmation, and sends your rep an order card ready to enter.
  6. Fifteen minutes before opening, the morning summary lands in your inbox and on WhatsApp.
